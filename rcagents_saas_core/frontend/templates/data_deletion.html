Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Your Data Deletion Rights

At RC Agents, we respect your privacy. If you have interacted with our platform through Meta APIs (Facebook Messenger, Instagram, or WhatsApp) and wish to request the deletion of your personal data, you can do so using one of the methods below.

We will process all verified deletion requests within 30 days and confirm once the deletion is complete.

2. Request Methods

2.1 Email Request

Send an email to contact@rcagents.space with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request". Please include the following information in your email:

  • Your full name
  • The platform you used (Facebook Messenger, Instagram, or WhatsApp)
  • Your Facebook / Instagram User ID (PSID) or WhatsApp phone number
  • The store name or domain you interacted with (if known)

2.2 Web Form

You can submit a deletion request using the form below. We will match your request against our records and process the deletion.

3. What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your request, we will permanently delete:

  • All conversation messages and chat history
  • Your platform User ID (PSID / Instagram ID / WhatsApp number)
  • Any stored profile information (name, profile picture, username)
  • Media files (images, documents) shared through conversations

4. Data We May Retain

Certain data may be retained if required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Order records: If you made a purchase, order details may be retained for tax and accounting purposes as required by Algerian law
  • Anonymized analytics: Aggregated, non-personal usage statistics
  • Legal holds: Data subject to ongoing legal proceedings or investigations

In all cases, retained data is stripped of personally identifiable information where possible and kept only for the minimum period required.

5. Deletion Confirmation

Once your data deletion request is processed, you will receive a confirmation email at the address provided in your request. The confirmation will include:

  • The date of deletion
  • The categories of data deleted
  • Any data retained and the reason for retention (if applicable)

6. Meta Platform Compliance

This Data Deletion endpoint complies with Meta Platforms' Platform Terms and Developer Policies requirements for App Review. We ensure that:

  • Meta-sourced user data is deleted upon request within 30 days
  • Users can request deletion without creating an account
  • Deletion applies to all data obtained through Meta APIs
  • Users are notified once deletion is complete
